OVERALL INTERPRETATION
======================================================================
Region 1 is the stronger provisional candidate based on its larger connected area, gentler mean slope, better separation from mapped GSI inventory points and retention under stricter distance thresholds.

Important concern: Region 1 has significant positive residual spatial autocorrelation in both InSAR pairs after plane removal, particularly Pair 2. This remains unresolved.

Region 3 has only 31 original pixels. Its one-layer interior contains five pixels, so interior statistics are weakly supported. Leave-one-out sensitivity was not tested for Region 3 in the available analysis.

Neither region is confirmed stable ground. The two InSAR pairs have different reference points, and raw LOS differences must not be interpreted as direct temporal displacement. Plane removal can also remove real broad deformation.

Recommendation: retain Region 1 as the primary provisional candidate and Region 3 as a secondary comparison region. Independent validation and additional temporally consistent observations are still required.
